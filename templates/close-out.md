# Close-out messages / 收尾消息

Send within a week of the verdict. Edit freely; keep them true.
结论出来后一周内发出。可以随意修改，但必须属实。

## If you build / 决定做

**EN**: Thanks for signing up for {offer}. Enough people said yes that we are building it. Expected opening: {date}. Your {deposit} stays refundable until then; reply "refund" any time. Reply "remove" to be taken off this list.

**中文**：感谢你报名 {offer}。愿意的人足够多，我们决定开始做了，预计 {date} 开放。在那之前，你的 {deposit} 随时可退，回复“退款”即可；回复“退出”即可从名单中移除。

## If you stop / 决定停

**EN**: Thanks for signing up for {offer}. We tested whether enough people wanted it, and the answer was no, so we are not building it. {Your deposit has been refunded to the original payment method.} We have deleted your details from this test. If you would like to hear what we learned, reply and we will tell you.

**中文**：感谢你报名 {offer}。我们测试了是否有足够多的人需要它，结果是不够，所以不会继续做。{你的定金已原路退回。}我们已删除你在这次测试中留下的信息。如果你想知道我们学到了什么，回复即可。

## If you reshape / 决定调整

**EN**: Thanks for signing up for {offer}. The idea is changing: {one sentence on what changes}. Nothing has been charged {or: your deposit is refunded}. If you want to hear about the new version, reply "yes"; otherwise we will delete your details by {date}.

**中文**：感谢你报名 {offer}。这个想法正在调整：{一句话说明改了什么}。我们没有扣任何费用{或：定金已退还}。如果想了解新版本，回复“要”；否则我们会在 {date} 前删除你的信息。
